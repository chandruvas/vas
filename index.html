How to Install Your VAS Email Signature

New Outlook

  1. Open Outlook.
  2. Click Settings (gear icon).
  3. Select Accounts → Signatures.
  4. Create a new signature.
  5. Enter a name for your signature.
  6. Click inside the signature editor.
  7. Press CTRL + V to paste your copied signature.
  8. Save your changes.

Classic Outlook

  1. Open Outlook.
  2. Select File → Options → Mail → Signatures.
  3. Click New and enter a signature name.
  4. Click inside the Edit Signature box.
  5. Press CTRL + V.
  6. Click Save and then OK.

Outlook on the Web

  1. Open Outlook in your browser.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Find Accounts → Signatures or Mail → Compose and reply.
  4. Create a new signature.
  5. Click inside the editor and press CTRL + V.
  6. Save your changes.
